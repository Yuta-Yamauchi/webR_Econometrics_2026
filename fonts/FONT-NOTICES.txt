Lecture MLModern Regular / Italic (derived web fonts)
Based on MLModern 1.2 (2021-01-18).
Copyright 2003-2009 B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki.
Copyright 2021 Daniel Benjamin Miller.
License: LPPL 1.3c or later, included as LPPL-1.3c.txt.
Complete original distribution: https://ctan.org/pkg/mlmodern

Changes: the outlines and advance widths from mlmr12.pfb, mlmmi12.pfb,
and mlmsy10.pfb are assembled into Unicode OpenType faces and encoded as
WOFF2. Regular Latin characters and digits use MLModern Roman 12;
italic Latin characters and Greek variables use MLModern Math Italic 12;
math operators use MLModern Math Symbols 10. Unicode aliases are supplied
for Greek mu, perpendicular, and mathematical italic Latin letters.
The derivative names, cmap, and OpenType metrics are generated by build_fonts.py.
This is a web text face, not a general-purpose OpenType MATH font.

The source Type 1 files are included in sources/ and the complete conversion
procedure is included in build_fonts.py. Rebuild with Python and fonttools[woff]:
python build_fonts.py PATH_TO_TEXLIVE_TEXMF_DIST

The original font authors are not responsible for this conversion.
